# Design assets

The current approved Android launcher and splash artwork is the K4/B Family Bevel
icon in [launcher-k4](launcher-k4/README.md). Its transparent master, deterministic
export script, circular-mask previews and separate full-square Google Play PNG
are retained for reproducibility. Use `scripts/generate_k4_icons.py` for this icon.

`launcher-frenchverbs.xml` and `launcher-template.xml` are historical vector masters;
they are no longer the application launcher. Do not run the historical launcher
generator to regenerate the current icon.

Official reusable Nice Education brand originals and provenance remain in
`nice-education/`. Preserve logo proportions and the official artwork.
